Our Core Work

We Can Help Children Survive!

The trust shelters and supports approximately 25 children, providing food and shelter, clothing, education and protection and moral support — so that in time each of them can secure employment and establish a stable future of their own.

That sentence is the whole of our work, and it is also the hardest part of it. A child who arrives here has usually lost more than a home. Getting them back on their feet is slow, daily and unglamorous: a hot meal that arrives on time, a uniform that fits, a school bag that is theirs alone, someone who notices when they are quiet. The rest of this page — the camps, the marriage services, the celebrations, the job placements — is the trust's work with the wider neighbourhood those children come from.

The Causes We Carry

Some of this work happens inside our own four walls. The rest happens out in the neighbourhoods of Bengaluru, wherever a family asks us for a hand.

Core Care

Shelter & Daily Care

Approximately 25 children live under our care. Food, a bed, clean clothes and an adult who knows their name — the ordinary things that stop being ordinary the moment a child loses them.

Free Community Mass Marriages

We arrange free community mass marriages for underprivileged families, with the help of Lions Club, Bengaluru — so that no family has to borrow against its future to give a daughter a dignified wedding day.

Three Ways You Can Help

None of this runs on goodwill alone. It runs on people who decide, on an ordinary Tuesday, to do one specific thing.

You do not need to commit to anything large. Come once, give once, send one message — we will take it from there and tell you honestly what is needed most.

Giving in kind? Ration, clothing, school books and stationery are always welcome. Please write to us before you send anything, so we can tell you what the children actually need right now — and so nothing goes to waste.

Donate Write to us instead
  1. Step 01

    Give

    A donation supports food and shelter, clothing, education and protection and moral support for the children in our care. You can transfer to the trust's bank account or scan one of its QR codes — both are on the donate page, with nothing hidden behind a form.

  2. Step 02

    Volunteer

    Sit with a child over their homework. Help register families at a health or eye camp. Roll up a sleeve at a blood donation drive. Lend a hand on Rajyothsava or Republic Day. Tell us what you are good at and when you are free, and we will find you a place.

  3. Step 03

    Partner

    Companies, clubs, colleges and hospitals can do what individuals cannot — host a camp, support a community mass marriage, or hire the trained women and youth we place into work. If your organisation wants to walk with us, start with a message.
